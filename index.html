What is included

  • FL-style production suite: 16/32/64-step Channel Rack with per-step velocity, pitch, pan, ratchets, and probability, zoomable Piano Roll with scale snapping, chord stamping, and .mid import/export, magnetic Playlist with razor splitting and automation lanes, and a full Console Mixer with stackable EQ, Compressor, Reverb, Delay, Saturation, Filter, and Chorus.
  • Built-in instruments & sound library: Dual-oscillator wavetable SubSynth, 4-operator 6-algorithm FMSynth, DrumSynth, Sampler, and RIFF/SF2 SoundFont player, pre-loaded with 4 studio 24-bit WAV drum kits, 4 wavetables, and a starter .sf2 bank.
  • JS & Python live coding: Quantized hot-reloading JavaScript (mini-notation, Euclidean rhythms, Markov chains, LFOs) and dual-mode Python (local Python 3 bridge plus embedded evaluator) with categorized Code Presets, Snippets drawer, inline AST pattern/piano-roll previews with isolated audition, and one-click Bake to Pattern or WAV.
  • Beat-reactive modulation & lossless mastering: Sample-accurate AudioWorklet clock, lock-free SharedArrayBuffer oscilloscope/FFT/LUFS/True Peak meters, 4-band frequency & envelope follower modulation matrix, and non-realtime 32-bit float / 24-bit / 16-bit WAV (TPDF & shaped dither), FLAC, AIFF, 320k MP3, OGG, and batch stem export.
